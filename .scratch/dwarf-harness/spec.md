# DwarfHarness: evidence-backed system specification

Status: ready-for-agent

Published: 2026-10-06; updated: 2026-10-08

Scope: Core, Router, Undercurrent and native GUI; design synthesis with D96's pre-alpha fortress alignment. This status does not imply exhaustive design closure or override approval, contract-freeze or implementation gates.

## Problem Statement

The owner needs a local, persistent colony of agents that can deliver real work without sacrificing private context, explicit authority, bounded spending or trustworthy evidence of completion. Parallel activity must not turn into a shared mind, an unbounded queue of model calls, or claims of success based on a model saying that work passed. Agents also need relationships, memory, roles and environmental awareness rather than only a central task queue.

The design is now distributed across four roadmaps and their supporting decisions, contracts and evidence. Implementers need one user-facing specification that joins those requirements, explains what must be observable, and keeps selected designs separate from pending approvals, contract freezes and unrun runtime or empirical gates.

## Solution

Build a Go-based local colony around one authoritative Core. Core owns durable history, execution authority, workspaces, validated promotion, tasks, grants, accounting and communication. The Router prepares and prices model requests without becoming a second authority. Undercurrent derives a bounded, deterministic environmental field from authorized evidence and exposes permission-scoped impressions rather than hidden content or instructions. Terminal and native GUI views control and inspect the same Core through its launcher contract.

Keep the colony’s social structure real: private contexts, durable relationships and conversations, Corps, parent and clone lineages, explicit birth, memory and Griot publication. Treat environmental perception as information an agent may use, never as permission to spend, interrupt, read private material or publish work. Federation remains separately governed and explicitly opt-in.

Implement in the canonical dependency order, with independently tested subsystem contracts and the required real end-to-end, native-interface and isolation gates. Preserve the modest-laptop and model-call-efficiency requirements without weakening the intended behavior. This specification consolidates the selected design; publication does not authorize blocked implementation.

Make the ordinary path easy to use correctly: authoritative mutations carry their own persistence and recovery, models get a small coherent tool surface with useful local argument errors, and the existing runtime owns continuation rather than leaving features to coordinate competing loops. Bounded background compaction should reduce foreground interruption without losing later progress or weakening context, authority or spending limits. These are the five non-GUI refinements adopted in [D88](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d88-make-the-ordinary-runtime-path-carry-correctness-and-model-facing-usability); their benefits remain to be measured.

As the colony owner, I want to stand in for a lost office under my own label and, if the officer cannot return, to declare it unrecoverable for a prepared, aligned Aspirant to take over with a handover Core assembles from records, so that no office goes vacant and no stand-in act is attributed to the officer.

## Implementation Decisions

### Cross-cutting boundaries

1. **One specification, unchanged authority.** The spec consolidates the four roadmaps and their adopted mechanisms. The current decision log, producer-owned specifications and contract register remain authoritative. Local publication and a ready-for-agent status do not grant D52 approval, freeze any of the six contracts or advance implementation readiness.

2. **Subsystem contract emphasis.** Organize acceptance around the real Core, Router and Undercurrent boundaries and their declared contracts, as selected by the owner. Keep the real end-to-end promotion, early private social/restart, native GUI and OS/VM qualification gates. Separate seams localize failures; they do not permit mock acceptance or substitute interface checks for effects outside those interfaces.

3. **Producer-ready additive freezes.** The six registered contracts are ledger-format, ledger-reader, ledger-appender, launcher-protocol, context-module and law-format. Their shared schemas use standard-library-only contracts. C1 initially freezes only specified C1-ready records; later producer-owned families require their real payload, permitted caller, consumers, privacy/replay fields and golden examples before owner freeze. An event catalogue entry does not mean its producer works, and an old summary is not permission to invent a missing mechanism.

4. **Evidence remains scoped.** Reuse the completed source reconciliation, judgment reviews and finite arithmetic/state experiments as design evidence, with their stated limitations. A document check, selected toolkit, model experiment, assurance label or passing speculative aggregate cannot establish production durability, privacy, isolation, native accessibility, workload capacity or empirical benefit. Each real acceptance gate must be run against its actual implementation and platform.

### Core runtime and authority

5. **Portable development boundary.** Use Go 1.27 with the pinned Go 1.27.2 toolchain (D106); keep Core and terminal cgo-free and state CGO_ENABLED for every build. Enforce the package import allowlist from the first owned package, use one check command for formatting, static analysis, vulnerability checks, import-boundary validation, tests and supported builds, and add dependencies only through the recorded approval process. C0 is configuration and proof, not placeholder packages.

6. **One durable operational colony ledger.** Use one append-only, typed, versioned colony ledger and one Core-owned serialized appender; operational records receive a gapless colony sequence, hash-chain identity and committed durability before acknowledgment. Store large bodies by content hash, preserve operational replay facts and command deduplication in authoritative segments, rebuild indexes, and make body collection explicit without deleting or compacting operational event history. Separate durable ledger cursors from ephemeral per-turn text deltas. Freeze only producer-ready families as each owner approves them. D90–D96 instead require bounded/replaceable presentation state and per-run cosmetic undo under decision 60, not immortal cosmetic-edit history or a second GUI store.

   **Compulsory Core transitions.** Make B3/B7's short, producer-owned typed Core transition the only mutation path for authoritative operational state, including new task state, workflow counters and pending work. It binds actor/context/operation/target, checks expected versions/current authority, publishes required private bytes and a bounded replay-complete event group through the existing sequencer, then updates rebuildable authorized projections and acknowledges the committed outcome. Callers cannot independently mutate authoritative records, append ad hoc state or remember separate persistence, projection and restart hooks. Each producer defines its operation and versioned replay/projection rules together; no universal entity framework or alternate operational store is introduced. Cosmetic current-state saves retain sole Core ownership and atomic/revision-checked acknowledgement through their separately specified bounded mechanism.

   Keep model, Git, checks and other expensive effects outside the short transition. Their existing durable intents and observed results return through typed, identity-bound transitions; an intent or command receipt is not proof that an external effect succeeded. Rebuilding state never dispatches work, and loss of an optional projection cache cannot lose committed state.

7. **Immutable host-observed checkpoints.** Use one dedicated context/workspace scratch and keep canonical main out of worktrees. Before capture, authenticate and deduplicate the request, reserve the per-lineage checkpoint and resource bounds, then either start exclusive host-managed capture immediately or return busy without a snapshot or queued retry. Keep K=4 distinct FIFO checkpoints per enrolled context/workspace pair, with workspace and colony capacity scaling as four times declared pairs; these are calibration bounds, not a colony-size limit. Capture a bounded full-tree manifest from Git-canonical contents under pinned capture inputs: tracked edits and deletions remain eligible, eligible untracked files respect captured scratch ignore rules, reviewed local/global exclusions stay pinned, and the mutable index is never authority. Preserve raw observation identity separately from the normalized candidate tree; reject unsupported conversions, collisions, special files, lossy normalization and cap exhaustion.

8. **Cumulative validation and ordered finalization.** Keep request, immutable submission, derived candidate, generation, validation attempt, finalization intent and accepted result as distinct identities. Derive successor deltas from their local parent's sealed tree while integrating over the foreign cumulative predecessor. Separately bound checkpoint admission and validation: K=4 non-coalescing checkpoints per enrolled pair, provisional P=4 validation lanes colony-wide and at most four per workspace, with a leading window of four prepared/running/result-held generations per workspace. Allow cumulative candidates to validate concurrently but publish only the fully validated successful prefix in durable request order. A failed or cancelled local parent invalidates its dependent suffix; removal of a foreign predecessor rebuilds surviving candidates and requires fresh full validation. A proven tree match with the actual durable predecessor yields no-change, not accepted delivery. Serialize durable intent, guarded ref mutation and durability, accepted ledger result, then slot release. Reconcile restart effects only from sufficient positive evidence; never reapply an uncertain Git update or automatically restart interrupted/queued work. Cancellation is request-specific, by its submitting context or colony owner, and becomes too late at durable intent.

9. **Owner-authorized policy activation.** Only the colony owner authorizes each exact validation-policy version. Core records and activates that retained version through one colony-wide transition, immediate or one-off scheduled with a not-before time; there is no recurring scheduler or per-workspace rollout. Hold new affected finalization intents, finish existing intents to proven durable outcomes, then switch policy. An unresolved effect delays activation. Refresh affected pre-intent lanes against changed policy, checks or environments using the original immutable submission; reuse a derived candidate only while its predecessor remains current. Stop superseded children and prove exit before replacement or resource reuse.

10. **Context-bound runtime and restart behavior.** Give each agent one resumable execution session with many private contexts; allow at most one active turn per context and provisionally at most three per agent, subject to workload measurement. Keep memory, handoff, transcript, continuation, approvals, routing/cache state and scratch bound to the context; the fixed active-context note names counterparts or task titles only. Publish private bytes durably before the ledger revision event and block/report any restart mismatch rather than guessing. Use expected-revision writes for agent-wide files, atomic Core records for assignment/reservation state, and a fair bounded ready-work scheduler. When a second same-agent turn first writes a task whose scratch is occupied, create temporary scratch from the task's latest commit, commit observed changes at turn end, and merge back when the task scratch is free; conflicts become work and the next task turn is notified. Credentials use explicitly configured terminal-environment or native OS-store sources under Core control. The GUI remains a display client, not a credential holder; never put secrets in the ledger, agent context or guest, and refuse dispatch when credentials are unavailable. Restart closes unmatched operations as interrupted and never blindly repeats tool effects.

    **One continuation owner.** The existing Core scheduler/runtime alone decides whether a bound turn continues, waits, yields or finishes. Tool results, social replies and finite workflows supply typed facts or requests to that owner, not independent loops or reminder flags. Persist the originating context, operation/generation, expected state, pending result identities, wait reason and applicable counters/deadline through the same Core transitions. This runtime state is distinct from provider continuation artifacts; neither follows view focus or another context.

    Apply precedence at each scheduling/dispatch boundary: an effective cancellation, shutdown or loss of required authority prevents new work; an exhausted deadline or finite loop bound takes its declared stop/escape; an unresolved required tool result, social reply or workflow branch prevents dependent dispatch; otherwise eligible continuation returns through ordinary fair scheduling and fresh authority/resource admission. A requested finish cannot skip pending required work or its evidence gate. Explicit social waits and workflow joins checkpoint/yield and release the worker and active-turn slot; an outstanding provider/tool call still belongs to its original turn until completion or a supported checkpoint. Waiting never releases unresolved financial holds or reuses unconfirmed child resources. Resume only on the matching authorized result or explicit command, not polling model calls, and never run two live turns in one context.

Respect operation-specific cancellation cutoffs and reconcile already-intended effects rather than claiming they were prevented. Promotion completion remains passive under D63 unless its original caller explicitly waits or another authorized trigger schedules a turn. Restart reconstructs waits and interrupted outcomes without automatically resuming execution. These rules belong to the existing runtime, not an extensible Director stack or new workflow engine.

Restart/reattach/viewing reconstructs progress, not a second paid first turn. Honor creation intent after approval: Create remains ready/inactive; Create and activate proceeds only through actual gates, without another mandatory Activate action.

Use authenticated C7 binding and safe argument-vector attachment to the actual colony/agent/session/context, not invented frozen CLI syntax, provider secrets or a fake console. Failure is explicit; detaching leaves Core alive.

### Service loss and recovery

64. **Record service loss once and keep Corps work inside existing scope.** Follow [Core §13.11](../../docs/DESIGN-CORE-AND-LAUNCHERS.md#1311-ordinary-service-loss-and-officer-recovery-d97d102) and [D97–D102](../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d97-officer-service-loss-causes-onset-held-questions-and-restoration-evidence). C3 records per-agent service loss with cause, onset and restoration for provider failure, exhausted owner money limits and execution failure; lost host contact joins when out-of-process execution exists. Onset follows a 2-minute grace counted from the router's pause, or from the first refused call before R1 exists; causes only the owner can clear count at once. No probe or model call detects or ends service loss: restoration is the agent's first completed call after the cause clears or stops applying, including after the owner moves all of the agent's work off the failed provider. R1 gives the owner one wait-or-stop prompt per cause with per-turn overrides, while switching provider and compacting stay per turn.

    C8 keeps a Corps working while its officer is lost. Further questions become reserved questions in the officer's queue, counted against a per-member allotment of three that each colony may configure; a member at its allotment pauses its ordinary work through the existing hold. Outage work is limited to claiming waiting tasks, splitting subtasks within the parent's criteria and at most three waiting task proposals per member. The owner may stand in for the office within its authority, labelled as the owner acting for it. On restoration Core sends the Service Restoration message in the officer's name at no cost and releases members in step. Succession pauses on Incumbent or Aspirant loss; the owner's unrecoverable declaration lets a prepared, aligned Aspirant take over with a handover Core assembles from records. Every office handover passes unanswered messages, pending decisions, proposals, the owner's office decisions and open tasks with fresh private task context; unsubmitted changes never carry over or submit automatically. C7 projects outages, pauses and stand-in acts to terminal and GUI by access. None of this makes C3 or base C7 wait for C8, C9 or GUI; record encodings and calibration remain producer work.

## Testing Decisions

1. **Subsystem contracts are the primary organizing seams.** The owner selected separate Core, Router and Undercurrent contract emphasis, retaining end-to-end acceptance. Test through each producer-owned public boundary and its observable results. Reuse the six declared contracts and real subsystem entry points; do not add test-only services, a parallel authority model or generic workflow machinery. Golden compatibility fixtures become authoritative only when their actual record families are specified and frozen.

2. **Assert behavior, not wiring.** A useful test fails on a plausible wrong permission, amount, durable effect, state transition, ordering, boundary condition or recovery result. Avoid assertions about private fields, copied values, mock echoes, incidental defaults, source text or prose wording. Keep fixtures deterministic and isolated; use fixed clocks, seeds and bounded inputs where the contract permits them. Use actual temporary stores, repositories and child processes when their effects are the subject of the test.

3. **Repository and platform integration proof.** When C0 is implemented, run the agreed check on the supported Windows, Linux and macOS configurations using the pinned Go/toolchain/compiler inputs and explicit cgo settings. Include the positive forbidden-import case, vulnerability-data prerequisites and the declared supported builds. Do not count an empty scaffold or a silently skipped platform check as that gate.

4. **Ledger format, reader and appender contracts.** Exercise version/family compatibility, absent fields, integrity and committed-record boundaries, incomplete suffixes, corruption, sequence cursors and replay. Verify that only permitted components can append, callers cannot choose sequence numbers, acknowledgement follows the defined durable commit, and replay does not issue execution, provider or external-tool work. Verify duplicate identity and old-ID lookup after hot-index eviction. Exercise shutdown and revocation reserve independence, same-class busy behavior, admission order and ordinary-work service after no more than two consecutive control commits while ordinary work waits.

   Exercise an implemented producer's accepted mutation, receipt, authorized read and crash/replay as one consumer-visible path. Interrupt before commit and after commit but before projection update/acknowledgement: only committed state survives, duplicate commands return the original outcome, and projections rebuild without another caller-specific recovery path. For external effects, separately interrupt around intent and observed result; replay may complete proven bookkeeping but must not manufacture success or repeat uncertain work.

5. **Law-format contract.** Use valid and adversarial law inputs to exercise strict typing, bounds, duplicate/unknown-field rules, exact immutable-byte identity and parser-profile limits. Test the distinction between shared law data and locally authorized checks, environments, grants and credentials. A valid parser result is not engine readiness or activation approval. Verify that C4 can consume the frozen validation-policy contract without requiring the field engine.

6. **Context-module contract.** Exercise authorized immutable snapshots, current audience and revocation checks, complete structured output, deterministic omission, per-invocation estimates and hard byte limits. Verify that the active-context note appears once, an unavailable or denied projection item cannot leak through ordering or fallback, and a non-fitting exploration row does not starve a later fitting row or become falsely marked seen. A token estimate is not a provider-tokenizer guarantee.

7. **Launcher-protocol contract.** Exercise authentication, version negotiation, framed input limits, typed commands, immutable same-ID replay and mismatch refusal, expected revisions, independent durable cursors, authorized snapshots and gap recovery. Test live text separately through atomic snapshot/high-water attachment and delta recovery. Disconnection or view closure must not create a second host, cancel work, replay commands automatically, or expose stale private content.

   Cosmetic save retries additionally exercise the producer's finite retention boundary: a retained same-ID save cannot duplicate a placement, an expired/stale command refuses without reapplication, and close/restart discards bounded undo but restores saved current layout/drafts/TODOs. Permanent operational receipts and append-only history keep their stronger retention; no test may pass by trimming birth/alignment/Purpose/publication/succession evidence.

8. **Core task, grant and broker behavior.** Through typed commands and resulting authorized views, exercise competing claims, stale assignments, review/failure callbacks bound to the exact task version and subject, duplicate results, revocation, budget scope and atomic task transitions. Boards and narratives cannot change authoritative task state. Distinguish accepted, delivered, processed and replied messages. Check private-context boundaries, recipient membership epochs, read-time authorization, retired delivered-history restrictions, clone lineage and context-scoped repairer exclusion.

   At the real model-tool entry point, a declared singleton/list equivalence must produce the same scoped operation and same-ID receipt; changing the target under that same command ID, an ambiguous form, a duplicate key or an undeclared coercion must not dispatch. A malformed request returns useful bounded local correction information without a repair-model call. Exercise catalogue discovery followed by revocation and invocation: inaccessible entries/targets never leak, stale discovered operations refuse, and discovery does not enlarge grants. Run strict law, ledger and launcher rejection cases at their own interfaces to show model-only tolerance has not spread into stored or protocol formats.

9. **Promotion and check-governance integration.** Use real temporary workspaces and the actual Core-owned runner to verify capture-or-busy, admission before capture, immutable submissions, local ancestry, foreign predecessor rebuilds, complete required-check evidence, request-order successful-prefix publication and exact accepted deltas. Exercise cancellation on both sides of durable intent, no-change only against the actual accepted predecessor, obsolete-child cleanup, failed local-parent suffixes and every interruption window around the serialized finalizer. Observe real Git state and durable acceptance together; a ref move or passing speculative aggregate is insufficient.

10. **Policy-activation transitions.** Exercise immediate and overdue scheduled requests, immutable same-ID binding, expected-revision replacement and cancellation, retained target artifacts, owner reauthorization, trusted-clock failure, stale rejection and release of only the request’s own hold. An unresolved intended finalization delays the shared switch; effective activation time is not backdated. Historical replay reconstructs state but never restarts execution.

11. **Router and paid-call boundaries.** Drive real request preparation and the controlled dispatch boundary with bounded deterministic provider fixtures where appropriate. Verify independent inclusive input/output maxima, exact rational upper-bound pricing with one final upward rounding, exclusive billing-field normalization, sealed semantic identity and credential-version binding, reservation before dispatch, durable intent before bytes, ambiguous-outcome holds and overrun admission blocking. Exercise missing usage, disconnects, unsupported capabilities, stale scoped headroom, retries as new authorized attempts, cache-write costs and compaction as paid work. Do not substitute a mock success for the Core’s actual admission/accounting behavior. Exercise optional cache changes on both sides of the downgrade-margin boundary; mandatory authority changes never wait for a cache benefit.

    For background compaction, start from an authorized immutable prefix, append a later exchange while the job runs, then adopt the result at a safe compilation boundary: the later exchange remains intact and canonical history is unchanged. Replace the covered prefix or summary base, revoke input access, or change to an incompatible binding before completion and verify stale output cannot publish. Exercise bounds, busy admission, interruption and unresolved paid outcomes; background work must not bypass foreground concurrency limits, create a second live context turn, retry automatically or switch providers. Compare subsequent task success and retained decision-relevant facts, not merely summary length or a returned success flag.

12. **Field and graph behavior.** Exercise deterministic replay, law and graph version binding, source replacement/withdrawal, evidence and assurance eligibility, directionality, floor-at-each-hop arithmetic, strongest-path ties, finite work and memory ceilings, arrival-time overflow, expiry and resumption. Multi-chunk graph updates expose wholly old or new versions, incremental results must agree with the declared rebuild, and unavailable analysis is unknown rather than empty. Exercise bounded ambient rejection and counter exhaustion without a biased fallback.

13. **Moods, sensing and sovereign federation.** Exercise deterministic keyed sensing without sample-only model calls, independent quota/money limits, durable notify-not-ask routing, two cooldown families, duplicate/crash closure, clock rollback and revocation. A repeated material condition must not reset cooldown or spending. Separately test bilateral peer/law/protocol/source grants, authenticated stream identity, duplicate-versus-mismatched facts, retained origin/assurance, revocation, disconnection as unknown coverage and rejection of transitive relay. Neither mood initiation nor an imported fact grants new authority.

Replay must not repeat work. This testing-only milestone can precede C4 completion and C5 retirement integration; it neither bypasses C4 nor completes all of C6.
