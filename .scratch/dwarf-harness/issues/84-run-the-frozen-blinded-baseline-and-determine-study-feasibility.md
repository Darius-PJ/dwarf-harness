### STUDY-BASELINE

Separate owner-approved Q26 protocol after exact-kind release. Four co-primary endpoints retained: duplicate minutes/all assigned eligible tasks (10% reduction); restricted mean time to confirmed repair through 30 days/pre-assignment admissible-failure risk set, unrepaired to horizon (10% reduction); validated success/all assigned tasks, including unsuccessful declines/failures/unavailable/incomplete (5-point increase); independently adjudicated useful discovery/all assigned tasks including zeros (5-point increase). Blinded run-in at least 20 independent clusters, two/stratum, measurable denominators/nonzero finite cluster variance for every endpoint, each binary outcome represented in >=5 clusters, >=20 admissible failures across >=10 clusters; extend only to 40 clusters. Invalid denominators, zero reduction baseline, impossible binary alternatives or insufficiency are inconclusive.
