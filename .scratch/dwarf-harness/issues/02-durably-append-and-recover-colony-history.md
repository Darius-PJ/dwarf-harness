## Answer

Ticket 02's share of C1 is built ([B18](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md#b18-c1-ledger-format-v1-encoding-colony-store-and-os-adapter)). `contract/ledger` drafts ledger format version 1 and its reader, `contract/ledger/appender` drafts the restricted append interface (D50), and Core's colony store `internal/core/ledgerstore` writes, locks and recovers a colony through the OS adapter `internal/core/durable`. One exclusive lock per colony survives a killed writer, appends get gapless sequences, and a commit counts as written only after its flush. A failed or uncertain write poisons the store until `Recover`. Opening a colony truncates only a torn final commit and fails closed on any earlier corruption, gap or unknown entry. Replay reads whole commits from a cursor with no effects, skips record types it does not consume and stops clearly on an unsupported version. On 2026-10-09, on Windows with go1.27.2, the full check passed including `-race`, the golden log regenerated byte-identically, three fuzz targets ran for 120 seconds each, and the concurrency, durability and killed-writer tests passed 20 times each. `golang.org/x/sys` became a direct dependency with the owner's sign-off (OG1). Ticket 03 adds the body store and body references and ends C1 with the F0 freeze request (OG2); Linux and macOS are only compiled until C3's CI exists.
