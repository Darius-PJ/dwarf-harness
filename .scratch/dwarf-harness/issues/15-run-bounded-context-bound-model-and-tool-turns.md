## Answer

Seven slices built the bound turn loop. S1 added record-free provider replay and tool mechanics: matched tool-call batches, observer and replay artifacts for both adapters, closed tool schemas and the model-tool argument normalizer, with an undeclared or malformed call carried as an untrusted request that settles and is scanned for the pinned credential. S2 added the F3-15 contract, six version-1 record types with schema-6 loop receipts and the chapter-15 golden, frozen by the project manager under D125 ([D165](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d165-f3-15-bound-turn-and-tool-records-frozen)); [D166](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md#d166-two-f3-15-producer-readings-refusals-before-a-native-command-and-unauthorized-passive-requests) adds two producer readings. S3 added the Core turn loop behind `internal/core/session`: one continuation owner with fair agent/context scheduling, per-call paid admission on the shared path, scoped file tools and the catalogue pair, durable F3-15 journaling, context-owned transcripts and fail-closed artifact replay. S4 added the terminal program, `cmd/dwarf-harness` over `internal/core/terminal`, with `Session.FinalText` for the durable answer and `CredentialScopeStatus` for startup binding; both are internal, unfrozen additions. The first criterion-8 run then stopped on an integrity fence, and a labelled diagnostic found two defects. S5 makes a busy finish park as `waiting_resource`, woken by a workspace release notifier, instead of stranding the turn. S6 closes the two-writer ledger race with a conditional submit at the expected tail (three attempts, then busy) for the paid, loop, review and route-configuration producers. In the rerun every turn that ended `content_invalid` was a paid attempt that had ended `unknown`; S7, a third extra slice the owner granted beyond D160's two, records an unknown paid result as `unresolved_result` and a `no_charge` result as `provider_error`, as the design brief's §6 says. The [ruling](../c3-evidence/ticket-15-design-ruling.md), the [fix ruling](../c3-evidence/ticket-15-fix-ruling.md), the [evidence](../c3-evidence/ticket-15-evidence.md) and the agents, providers, session and terminal package documentation give the detail.

Limits: criterion 8 is met for three of the four preregistered sizes. The rerun on aebcc11 stopped at its four-hour bound during the third cell's fresh `Open`, 20×20×3 did not run, and the full matrix waits for ticket 142. Its figures are loopback mechanics under the whole-history-scan cost profile the diagnostic measured; the first run failed on the two-writer race before the fixes. The saturation probes do not exercise the busy rule at these sizes: the transport timed out the driver's held responses before the probes ran, and the project manager's ledger check shows the busy rules held at every probe; `TestTurnLoopAdmissionAndFairness` proves the rule. Paid attempts that end `unknown` keep their holds until reconciliation, which is the held money in the cells. The 20×20×1 integrity verdict comes from the project manager's ledger scan, not from a fresh `Open`. Exchange-token and resource waits are contained in the server-side figures and cannot be separated under the ruled observer. The live drill was OpenAI only (`gpt-6-luna`, one turn, two calls, one tool) and ran through a Session in a test driver, not the terminal program; replay of Anthropic thinking and redacted thinking and of OpenAI encrypted reasoning is proven on loopback only, and the static route sends no thinking or effort controls. A MergeBack failure withholds a finished answer (failed, `scratch_pending`) while its text objects persist. One workspace release notifier serves one `workspace.Service`, so one Core runs per colony. Each launch binds a fresh credential version; versions accumulate up to `MaxCredentialVersions` unless `revoke_previous_version` names one. `PendingLoops` and `recovery_pending` are metadata only until ticket [16](16-prove-concurrent-private-runtime-restart-and-child-shutdown.md).
