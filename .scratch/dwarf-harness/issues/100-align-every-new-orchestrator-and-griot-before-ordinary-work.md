## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before finalization the creator is warned that even Create-only immediately starts the first alignment turn and incurs model usage; the warning grants no spend, and refused or pending admission is shown rather than hidden.
- [ ] Every new Orchestrator's and Griot's first action is the pinned run in its domain (Orchestrator: expected work; Griot: values) against the shared Corps or Colony Purpose, ordered Orchestrator then Griot for team onboarding; the first turn starts right after creation and then waits without unattended rounds.
- [ ] Only the human accepts initial alignment, whenever they judge it reached, even with questions open; a superior agent or the interviewer cannot accept, and guidance recommends stopping when questions no longer feel consequential.
- [ ] A new Corps' ordinary work waits until both leaders are accepted; inspection, alignment and recorded message receipt continue, and messages, assignments or reconnects cannot bypass the hold. Adding workers to an aligned Corps restarts nothing.
- [ ] After acceptance, Create leaves the team ready but inactive, and Create and activate proceeds when grants, budgets and operational gates permit, without another Activate step.
- [ ] Restart, reattach and two terminal views resume the same run and decision without a duplicate first turn or spending; agent-born leaders follow the same path in addition to their birth approval.
