## Answer

Core's agent runtime now restarts without resuming execution, and shutdown of the Git children it starts is positive. The work followed a fresh Fable ruling with 20 amendments and five binding clarifications, in four slices; S3's redesign had its own ruling.

S1 froze F3-16 (D169): two version-1 `runtime.` records through which one continuation owner per bound turn persists its state (the F3-16 catalogue in [`docs/CONTRACTS.md`](../../../docs/CONTRACTS.md) and the F3-16 section of the [`contract/ledger` documentation](../../../contract/ledger/doc.go)). S2 built that owner in `internal/core/agents`: each Core transition persists a park before waiting and a resume before the next effect; `Open` stays inert and only explicit `ReconcileLoop` reconciles; Session cancellation returns a durable stop acceptance, and the first durable stop or a committed native ending wins. Clarification 2 lets a turn write a same-agent sibling task's scratch, which reaches D23's temporary worktree; reads and other agents stay denied. The [package documentation](../../../internal/core/agents/doc.go) states the rules.

S3 added `internal/core/gitprocess` (D168). Every workspace Git child runs under an `Owner`; on Windows each runs in a named Job whose name and root identity are durable before it resumes, closure (an empty Job and process list, joined pumps) is proved before reuse, and anything unproved is retained and refused until the next start reconciles it. Its first form doubled the hosted Windows check through durable writes per child; under D171 the redesign keeps every proof with one flush per child: 32 never-reused Job names reserved per batch in one container, and a write-once root frame synced before resume. The [package documentation](../../../internal/core/gitprocess/doc.go) states the protocol and its residuals.

S4 added the passive-result consumer: a temporary's merge result reaches its original context's next natural turn through the private `owner_input` schema 2 and the F3-16 notice cursor, with no completion-only call. It also added the integrated rows, the executable's shutdown and startup rows and a bounded shutdown join that releases a failed parked turn for the next start's reconciliation; the measurement driver and analyzer it built are kept outside the repository. The campaign reports figures, not bounds or capacity; its matrix stopped at its preregistered 12-hour cap before the two 60-context cells, and by the owner's decision (D173) the profiles are met here within that cap while ticket [146](146-explain-why-turns-queue-with-many-active-contexts-and-measure-the-60-context-profiles.md) explains the queueing the matrix showed and then measures those cells. F3-16 and every golden are unchanged after the freeze.

This makes no C3 Done claim: tickets 90, 91 and 146 remain, and C3's status is stated only in its [Status line](../../../docs/ROADMAP-CORE.md#c3-agent-runtime) and [board row](../../../docs/ROADMAPS.md#status-board).
